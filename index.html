How it works

Every video is rendered by a program: simulations of functions fighting, growth races, sorting and path-finding algorithms, curves drawn by Fourier series, puzzles solved by backtracking. No AI-generated footage. The program picks a format, renders it, checks it and publishes it to the channels above twice a day.
