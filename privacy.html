Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026

"satisfying-math-poster" is a private, automated publishing tool used only by the owner of the Satisfying Math channels (Instagram @satisfying.math and YouTube @satisfying.math.shorts). It is not offered to other people and has no other users.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube, through the YouTube API Services: it uploads videos generated by its own code to the Satisfying Math channel, and reads that channel's public statistics (views, likes, comments) and YouTube Analytics reports (watch time, average percentage viewed, shares, subscribers gained).
  • Instagram, through the Instagram Graph API: it publishes Reels to @satisfying.math and reads that account's insights.

It only accesses the Satisfying Math accounts themselves. It does not read, collect or store personal data about viewers, commenters or any other users.

How the data is used and stored

The statistics above are saved in a private repository and used only to decide which kinds of videos to make next. They are not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Access tokens are kept as encrypted secrets and are never published.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By watching Satisfying Math videos on YouTube you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google Account connections page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to @satisfying.math on Instagram.
